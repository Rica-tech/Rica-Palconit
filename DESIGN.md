## Brand & Style
The brand targets mid-to-large general contractors, project managers, and civil engineering consultancies across the United States and Australia. The persona projects absolute structural integrity, procedural discipline, mathematical accuracy, and seamless remote operational support. 

The aesthetic is **Architectural Modernism with Technical Drafting Precision**:
- Grounded in high-trust engineering discipline rather than generic tech SaaS visuals.
- Infused with drafting cues: subtle coordinate markers, fine hairline grid guides, structural alignment lines, and clean geometric delineations.
- Emotionally evokes reliability, field competence, regulatory fluency (US OSHA/CSI MasterFormat alongside AS/NZS codes), and executive-level virtual assistant capability.
- Balances high-density technical data (estimates, takeoff metrics, schedule deltas) with spacious, executive-facing clarity.

## Colors
The palette bridges technical blueprint aesthetics with job-site industrial vigor:

- **Primary Blueprint Navy (`#0F2942`)**: Anchors visual authority, core structural frames, headlines, primary actions, and hero canvas treatments. Evokes traditional architectural blueprints and boardroom professionalism.
- **Secondary Industrial Safety Amber (`#D97706`)**: Reserved strictly for high-priority calls to action, active project milestones, critical metrics callouts, and interactive focus states. Evokes site safety vests, high-visibility equipment, and precision alerts.
- **Tertiary Architectural Slate (`#475569`)**: Used for structural borders, secondary typography, technical legends, table column headers, and subdued diagrams.
- **Neutral Steel Gray (`#64748B`)**: Manages meta labels, coordinate stamps, and subtle structural grid rules.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`)**: Ultra-clean, field-spec paper backdrop providing sharp contrast for high-density documentation and CAD/PDF preview embeds.

## Typography
The typographic architecture utilizes a tri-tier hierarchy:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an engineered, tech-forward, draftsman posture. The geometric proportions mirror architectural CAD titling without sacrificing digital polish.
- **Body (`Inter`)**: Delivers neutral, tall x-height legibility across dense bid specifications, task logs, and narrative bios.
- **Technical & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Carries drafting tags, standard compliance indicators (`AS/NZS 1170`, `OSHA 1926`, `CSI Div 03`), timestamps, coordinate notations, and software capability pills.

## Layout & Spacing
A structured 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile. Maximum container width is constrained to `1280px` to maintain strict readable column lines and eliminate wide-canvas drift.

- **Grid Alignment**: Outer sections feature hairline engineering boundary lines (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) with faint coordinate tick marks at section intersections (`+` intersections).
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows an 8px architectural grid. Component layouts prioritize clear visual partitioning—pairing high-density data matrices (e.g., estimating breakdowns) alongside breathing room for key project value propositions.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop`: ≥1024px (12 cols, 24px gutters, 48px canvas margin)
  - `Tablet`: 640px–1023px (6 cols, 16px gutters, 24px canvas margin)
  - `Mobile`: <640px (4 cols, 16px gutters, 20px canvas margin)

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through clean surface stratification and architectural boundary lines rather than heavy blur shadows:

- **Low-Contrast Technical Outlines**: Elements use crisp `1px` structural borders in `#CBD5E1` (on white) or `#334155` (on navy containers).
- **Subtle Drafting Shadows**: Hover states and active cards utilize zero-blur or micro-offset shadows with amber or navy wash:
  - Surface resting: No shadow; hairline border `#E2E8F0`.
  - Surface interactive hover: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
  - Floating CTA / Drawer: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.16)`.
- **Engineering Blueprint Inversion**: Primary conversion modules and calendar schedule modules use dark Blueprint Navy (`#0F2942`) containers layered over clean paper grounds for authoritative focal contrast.

## Shapes
Sharp, controlled geometric tailoring (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radius). This reflects drafting templates, spec binders, and laser-cut tolerances:

- Buttons, form controls, and technical badges leverage compact `4px` radiuses.
- Modal panels and core portfolio case study cards step up to `8px` (`rounded-lg`).
- Never use pill (`9999px`) shapes for buttons or inputs, maintaining the firm, stable persona of structural engineering.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Site Direct / Hire CTA)**: Solid Industrial Safety Amber (`#D97706`) background, white text, 4px corner radius, bold font weight. Interactive hover shifts to `#B45309` with a subtle 1px translate up.
- **Secondary (Spec / Resume Download)**: Transparent fill, 1.5px solid Blueprint Navy (`#0F2942`) outline, Blueprint Navy typography. Hover fills to `#0F2942` with white text.
- **Technical Ghost (RFI / Case Study Links)**: Flat, inline text paired with an angled engineering arrow (`↗`) and `JetBrains Mono` tracking.

### Capability Badges & Software Tags
- Software badges (AutoCAD, Revit, Bluebeam, Procore, PlanGrid, CostX, MS Project): Architectural Slate border (`#CBD5E1`), crisp off-white fill (`#F1F5F9`), `JetBrains Mono` label font.
- Regulatory Badges (US OSHA 30, CSI MasterFormat, AS/NZS 1170, NCC Compliance): Tinted blueprint badge (`rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.05)` fill, `#0F2942` text, left 2px solid indicator bar in Safety Amber).

### Project Metric & Takeoff Cards
- Bounded by hairline borders with an upper technical header row (e.g., `REF: TK-2024-AU`).
- Metric value rendered in `Space Grotesk` display-size typography, followed by unit label and sub-metric description (e.g., `±0.8% Estimate Accuracy across $14M AU Civil Works`).

### Experience & Project Timeline
- Vertical left-hand hairline drafting axis with solid anchor nodes at each role/contract.
- Date ranges displayed in monospaced format with dual-timezone badges (`AEST / US EST`) to reassure international remote operational coverage.

### Booking & Consultation Module
- Blueprint Navy surface with crisp high-contrast white form inputs, time slot selectors styled as toggle grids, and clear operational parameters (turnaround times, software licenses held, remote hardware specs).
